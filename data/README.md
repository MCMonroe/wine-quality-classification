# Data

`winequality-red.csv` (1,599 wines) and `winequality-white.csv` (4,898 wines)
come from the [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/186/wine+quality),
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The
original files are semicolon-delimited; these copies are comma-delimited but
otherwise unchanged.

Each row is one wine. The 11 predictors are physicochemical lab measurements,
and `quality` is the median of at least three blind expert ratings on a 0–10
scale.

Source: Cortez, P., Cerdeira, A., Almeida, F., Matos, T., & Reis, J. (2009).
Modeling wine preferences by data mining from physicochemical properties.
*Decision Support Systems, 47*(4), 547–553. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dss.2009.05.016
